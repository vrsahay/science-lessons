You now understand Newton's first law: with zero net force, an object at rest stays at rest and a moving object keeps a constant velocity, with zero acceleration. A push equal to friction keeps motion steady, and with no net force the position-time graph is flat (at rest) or a rising straight line (constant velocity), while the velocity-time graph is horizontal.
