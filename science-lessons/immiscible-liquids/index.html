Separating Immiscible Liquids

Pour cooking oil into a glass of water. Stir. Walk away for a minute. When you return, the two liquids have already drawn an invisible line between them — oil floating cleanly on top, water below, refusing to mix no matter how hard you shake. They are immiscible: they live in the same container but they keep their distance. So how do you scoop one out without taking any of the other? With a clever piece of glassware called a separating funnel — and a tap at the bottom you can open just enough to let one layer slip out while the other stays behind.
