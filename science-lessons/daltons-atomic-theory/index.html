Dalton’s Atomic Theory

In 1808, an English schoolteacher named John Dalton wrote down six elegant postulates about the nature of matter — and changed chemistry forever. Atoms, he said, are tiny indestructible particles that rearrange but never vanish. Let’s meet Dalton, walk through his postulates, and see them in action with real reactions!

You’ve mastered Dalton’s Atomic Theory! You met John Dalton, the English schoolteacher who proposed six elegant postulates in 1808.
